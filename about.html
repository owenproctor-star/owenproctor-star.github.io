About Owen Proctor

Licensed Pharmacist · Colorado

Who Am I

I was born and raised in Denver, Colorado, and I'm proud to call it home. I'm a licensed pharmacist with several years of experience, but there's a lot more to me than what I do for work.

I'm a husband, proud dog dad, and lifelong Broncos and Avalanche fan (for better or worse). I've always been a bit of a nerd when it comes to technology, gaming, and figuring out how things work. Whether it's messing around with my 3D printer, working on a project around the house, or going down a rabbit hole learning something completely random, I usually have something I'm tinkering with.

I enjoy traveling, exploring new places, spending time with my wife and our dogs, and finding new hobbies to keep things interesting. I'd consider myself pretty laid-back, curious, and someone who appreciates good humor and doesn't take himself too seriously.

I'm always looking to learn something new, try something different, and hopefully have a little fun along the way.
